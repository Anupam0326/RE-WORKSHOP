## Platform

## Users

Two audiences, equally important (confirmed 18 Oct 2026):
- People in Jabalpur who could come to the open-kitchen millet café on Ridge Road, South Civil Lines.
- Health-conscious shoppers who want traditionally processed organic staples and order them through WhatsApp.

Both arrive mostly on phones, often from Google, Instagram or a WhatsApp share. The first job is to understand what Re Workshop is and choose: visit the café, or shop the store.

A third, smaller audience since 31 Oct 2026 (client): farmers who farm without chemicals, want to switch, or keep native seed varieties, and want to connect with Re Workshop ("For farmers" page, enquiries to the store's WhatsApp). What Re Workshop offers them is not decided yet, so the page promises nothing; most of them will read Hindi more easily than English.

## Product Purpose

Re Workshop is an organic food store and an open-kitchen millet café in Jabalpur, presented as a premium lifestyle brand (owned by Resoilfy Agro Farms Pvt. Ltd.). The website explains the soil-to-plate idea, brings people to the café, and lets them build a basket of groceries that is sent as a WhatsApp order. Success: café visits and WhatsApp orders.

## Positioning

Soil to plate, in the brand's own words: "We don't just sell organic food. We restore life." Food grown with Amrit Krishi regenerative farming on Amrit Mitti soil, processed slowly (stone chakki, kacchi ghani wood presses), cooked in an open kitchen you can watch, under a No-Compromise Code (zero maida, refined oil, processed white sugar, corn starch). The name carries the promise: Re = restoring soil, repairing health, returning to roots, regaining nutrition, reviving the traditional Indian kitchen; Workshop = broken food mended, not thrown away.

## Product Principles

1. Brand before basket: earn belief in soil to plate before asking anyone to buy.
2. Two doors, equal weight: the café visit and the grocery order are both first-class actions.
3. Only true things: every claim, number and photo must be real or clearly marked as a placeholder.
4. Local and specific: Jabalpur, Ridge Road, Amrit Mitti, the open kitchen; never generic "organic" imagery.
